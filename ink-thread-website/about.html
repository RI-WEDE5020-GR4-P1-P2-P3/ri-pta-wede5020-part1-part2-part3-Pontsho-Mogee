About the Studio

Our Story

Ink & Thread Studio was founded on a simple idea: everyone has a story worth printing. What started as a small passion for design and textiles grew into a studio dedicated to helping individuals, students and small businesses turn their ideas into physical, wearable and giftable products.

Our Creative Philosophy

We believe personalisation matters. Rather than offering generic, mass-produced items, we work closely with each customer to understand their idea, refine it, and bring it to life through custom textile and graphic design.

Mission

To transform creative ideas into personalised, high-quality visual and textile products.

Vision

To become a recognisable youth-focused creative studio known for originality, personalisation and quality.
