How It Works

From idea to finished product in four simple steps.

1. Submit Your Idea

Tell us what you're looking for using our design request form.

2. Discuss the Design

We chat through your idea, colours, sizing and any reference material.

3. Approve the Design

We prepare a design proof for you to review and approve before printing.

4. Create & Collect

Once approved, we produce your item and arrange collection or delivery.

Common Questions

Turnaround depends on the product and the quantity. Once a design is approved, most items are ready within a few working days. Use the enquiry form to ask for an estimate for your order.

Send your artwork as a PNG, JPG or PDF. Larger, high-resolution files print more clearly. If you only have a small image, tell us in the design description and we will check it before printing.

Small changes can be made before printing starts. Once an item is in production, changes may not be possible, so please check your design proof carefully before approving it.

You can choose to collect your order or have it delivered. We will confirm the delivery options and costs once your order is approved.
